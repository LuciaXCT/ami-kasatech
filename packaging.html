Caption — post 01, launch

Meet AMI, the focus buddy.
Twenty-five minutes, then a break. It nudges you to rest your eyes, unclench your shoulders, sip some water. It even leaves a tiny treat for break time: a joke, a riddle, a small story.

Caption — post 02, the name

Why Kasa? Because kasa means house. We write it with a K, the way you say it. No dashes, no dots, no invented symbols. A house is simple, warm, and holds the things that matter. That is how we build.

AMI is for everyone who sits down to do a thing. Students starting at a blank page, nurses studying after a shift, anyone working from a kitchen table. Grid logic

Nine posts: object, words, detail. Left column carries the product, middle column carries the sentence, right column carries the proof. Read down, not across.
  • One idea per frameIf removing it leaves the idea intact, it is not in the post.
  • No emoji, everTypography and the object carry it. Badges and arrows are removed.
  • Two hashtagsReal ones, lowercase, at the end. No stacks of twenty.
  • No health claimsNever "cures", never "treats". It is a desk companion, not a device.
